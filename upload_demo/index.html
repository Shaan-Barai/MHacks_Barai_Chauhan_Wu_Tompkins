3. What was left over

Pixels wasted counts visible leftover-food pixels in AI-generated masks. It is not grams, servings or a percentage of the original meal. CO2 and water points are relative: they let a pixel of chicken count for more than a pixel of rice, but they are not kilograms or litres. Points = pixels ÷ 1000 × the food's grams per cm² × its footprint per kg.
